Common questions
Frequently asked.
How does a borehole project begin?
It begins with an enquiry, followed by a site assessment to determine project scope and equipment requirements.
Is a site assessment required?
Yes — an assessment helps determine the most suitable approach for your site.
Can you install pumps and piping?
Yes. We install borehole and submersible pumps, piping, storage and pressure systems.
Can an existing borehole be deepened?
Existing boreholes can be assessed for deepening depending on site conditions.
Do you repair borehole systems?
Yes — we diagnose, service and repair existing borehole and water systems.
Do you supply borehole equipment?
Yes — pumps, pipes, casing, pressure tanks, storage tanks, cables and accessories.
